About

USA National Parks Explorer is a simple, evergreen directory of all 63 U.S. national parks. Descriptions are short, original summaries written for planning — they are not copied from the National Park Service. For fees, hours, road closures, permits, and safety alerts, always check the official NPS page linked for each park. This site is not affiliated with the National Park Service.
